About Charity Aid YCO

Empowering Communities, Transforming Lives through sustainable development, economic empowerment, education support, environmental stewardship, health promotion, and humanitarian assistance.

Who We Are

Charity Aid YCO is a nonprofit and community-centered organization committed to reducing poverty, promoting human dignity, and empowering communities to achieve sustainable social and economic wellbeing. We work collaboratively with communities, institutions, development partners, and stakeholders to create lasting solutions to development challenges.

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Our Vision

A society where every individual and community has equal opportunities to achieve sustainable social and economic wellbeing.

Our Mission

To empower vulnerable populations through sustainable development, education, healthcare, economic opportunities, community participation, and strategic partnerships.

Core Values

Integrity, Transparency, Accountability, Inclusivity, Innovation, Community Participation, Sustainability, Respect, and Partnership.

Why We Exist

Poverty Alleviation

Supporting vulnerable households with sustainable solutions that improve their livelihoods and resilience.

Community Empowerment

Promoting local ownership, leadership development, and active participation in community transformation.

Sustainable Development

Creating long-term social, economic, and environmental impact through strategic and inclusive interventions.

Strategic Implementation Plan (2027-2032)

The Strategic Implementation Plan provides a roadmap for organizational growth, community empowerment, and sustainable impact over a five-year period.

Phase I - 2027

Institutional Establishment and Foundation Building

Phase II - 2028

Program Launch and Community Engagement

Phase III - 2029

Organizational Growth and Economic Empowerment

Phase IV - 2030

Sustainability and Expansion

Phase V - 2031-2032

Impact Consolidation and National Positioning

Our Commitment

Charity Aid YCO is committed to accountability, transparency, community participation, and measurable results. We strive to become a nationally recognized organization that contributes meaningfully to poverty reduction, social equity, environmental sustainability, and community transformation through innovative and inclusive development programs.
