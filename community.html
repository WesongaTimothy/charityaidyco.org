Together We Grow

A Community Built on Unity, Opportunity and Support

At Charity Aid YCO, we believe lasting development begins when people come together. We encourage communities to organise, save collectively, exchange knowledge, develop skills, mentor one another and support families facing challenges. Our vision is to nurture connected, resilient and self-reliant communities where everyone has an opportunity to participate and thrive.

What We Encourage

Growing Together Through Community Action

Six interconnected approaches that help build stronger, more inclusive and self-reliant communities.

01 · Financial Inclusion

Merry-Go-Round & Savings

Encourage members to contribute regularly, take turns receiving pooled contributions and develop a culture of saving. These groups help members plan for household needs, emergencies and future goals.

02 · Collective Growth

Chamas & Table Banking

Promote organised community groups where members pool resources, build financial discipline, explore investments and support one another's economic aspirations.

03 · Lifelong Learning

Education & Knowledge Sharing

Create opportunities for community members to learn through awareness sessions, financial literacy, life skills, educational support and knowledge exchange.

04 · Personal Development

Mentorship & Leadership

Connect experienced community members, professionals and volunteers with young people and emerging leaders to share experience, guidance, skills and inspiration.

05 · Social Solidarity

Mutual Support & Solidarity

Encourage neighbours and community groups to stand together during difficult times, support vulnerable households and strengthen a culture of compassion.

06 · Peace & Inclusion

Harmony, Inclusion & Togetherness

Promote peaceful coexistence, respect, equality, dialogue and inclusion. Build communities where people value diversity and work together for the common good.

Financial Empowerment

Saving Together. Growing Together.

Small contributions, when organised collectively, can create opportunities for individuals, families and communities. We encourage responsible community savings initiatives built on trust, transparency and shared responsibility.

↻

Merry-Go-Round

Members contribute an agreed amount at regular intervals, with the pooled amount rotating among members according to an agreed schedule.

♧

Chama & Table Banking

Members pool their savings to build collective financial strength, access agreed group lending arrangements and pursue shared investment goals.

✓

Financial Literacy

Promote budgeting, responsible borrowing, record keeping, financial planning and accountable group leadership.

Important: Community savings belong to participating members and are managed according to their agreed group rules. They are separate from charitable donations to Charity Aid YCO.
