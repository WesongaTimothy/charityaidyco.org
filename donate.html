Donate Goods & Supplies

Not everyone can donate financially, but everyone can make a difference. Charity Aid YCO welcomes in-kind donations that support orphans, children with disabilities, vulnerable children, youth, women and disadvantaged families.

👕

Clothing Donations

New or gently used clothes for orphans, children with disabilities, vulnerable children, youth and families in need.

🎒

School Uniforms

School uniforms, shoes, sweaters, sportswear, socks and other essential school items for vulnerable learners.

📚

Books & Learning Materials

Textbooks, storybooks, revision materials, dictionaries and educational resources that improve learning outcomes.

Exercise books, pens, pencils, rulers, mathematical sets, crayons and other classroom supplies.
